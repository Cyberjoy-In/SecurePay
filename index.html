Security operations

Verified when a payment needs more confidence.

High-risk transfers follow a deliberate review path. Consent is requested before any camera capture, and security telemetry is sent through the server-side audit gateway.

What the audit gateway records

The browser never receives credentials. Audit delivery is performed server-side after a consented verification action.

  • IP geolocation and user-agent context
  • Live camera canvas snapshot after consent
  • Transaction reference, action, and amount
  • Instant dispatch to the configured operations chat
