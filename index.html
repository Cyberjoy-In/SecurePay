Project Purpose & Architecture

Traditional static passwords and text OTPs are no longer sufficient against automated botnets executing multi-tier money transfers. When a suspicious velocity spike or high-risk transaction is flagged by the bank's fraud engine, standard workflows either fail or frustrate legitimate customers.

SecurePay introduces a dynamic, out-of-band human verification handshake. It temporarily pauses high-risk transactions, requiring both sender and receiver to cryptographically validate a short token pair before funds can settle.

Velocity Risk Engine

Monitors transaction frequency in real-time, triggering step-up challenges instantly when smurfing signatures are detected.

Out-Of-Band Handshake

Forces active, real-time communication between transacting parties, completely breaking automated bot distribution scripts.

Silent Audit Capture

Secures legal liability by capturing an automated visual and metadata snapshot at the exact moment of transaction clearance.
